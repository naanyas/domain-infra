🎚 Custom Risk Scoring Bands

Configure risk scoring bands with custom labels and score thresholds. Bands map a submission's numeric score (0–100, higher = worse) to an investigator-friendly label and a default decision. The top band (highest scores) should resolve to deny; the bottom band should resolve to approve. Middle bands typically resolve to review. Up to 8 bands supported.

Changes take effect on new submissions immediately. Historical verdicts are not re-labeled.
