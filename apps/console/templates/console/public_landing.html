About this engine

What does it actually check?

SDAT v2 is the successor to the Streamlit "Config Checker" prototype. The initial version of this engine was installed into a prominent email provider's infrastructure for live onboarding-fraud detection. v2 wraps that proven analyzer in a multi-tenant Django + Postgres API, with cross-customer fingerprint reputation and a community feedback loop on the way.

  • DNS & email authentication

    SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MX, A, PTR. Reverse-DNS sanity check.

  • TLS & web presence

    HTTPS validity, certificate status, redirect chains.

  • Threat intel & reputation

    VirusTotal, multiple DNSBL/RBL feeds, hacklink keyword sweep.

  • Phishing & impersonation

    Brand-spoofing, typosquatting, credential forms, known phishing-kit signatures.
